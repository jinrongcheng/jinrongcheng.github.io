01 / COMMUNITY & YOUTH
Y.

Youth Impact
Network of Canada

A student-led network connecting young people with charities and practical opportunities to contribute. Its book work has included distribution of teen and young adult books through event tables and partner organizations, van pickups, donations to The Children's Book Bank, and a book drive that collected 1,576 books. Across its campaigns it has collected and distributed 15,000+ books and 7,000+ cans of food, with 4,000+ participating students and 4+ participating schools, coordinated by a 25+ person student team.
