I am a Toronto high school student, and most of my week is spent doing the unglamorous parts of politics.

At the Willowdale constituency office, I help residents get straight answers from government and keep track of the cases that come in. With the Youth Impact Network of Canada, I help get free books from First Book Canada to teenagers in Toronto and North York. On a Ward 19 campaign, I knock on doors and work on housing and food costs. I like the follow-up work. The notes. The next call. The thing that actually gets done.

01 / THE THROUGH LINE

I did not start with a theory. I started with a constituent file.

One file turned into a hundred, then a thousand. A doorstep conversation turned into a policy question. A committee room turned into a weekend of prep. That is how I learned how this stuff actually works. Not from a textbook. From doing the next small job well.

02 / WHAT I AM DOING RIGHT NOW

Not a platform.
Just the work so far.

01

Constituency work

Willowdale. I track cases, research stakeholders, write briefing notes, and show up at community events. The job is mostly listening carefully and following through.

02

Books, not speeches

YINC. Right now we are moving more than 1,000 teen and young adult books from First Book Canada out through event tables and partner organizations in Toronto and North York.

03

Doorsteps and campaigns

Ward 19. I canvass, write, and work on policy for housing affordability, food insecurity, and cost of living. Small campaigns do not have a data team, so you learn fast what actually lands at a door.
