What this site collects

The site itself has no forms, no newsletter sign-ups, no accounts, and no advertising, so the site owner does not directly collect, store, or sell your personal information when you visit. However, the site uses Google Analytics, which collects anonymized usage information (such as pages visited and approximate location) via cookies, under Google's own privacy policy. You can opt out through Google's opt-out tools or your browser settings.

Cookies

This site uses Google Analytics cookies, but only if you accept them in the cookie banner that appears on your first visit. If you decline, analytics runs without storing analytics cookies. Your choice is saved in your browser so the banner does not keep reappearing. Cloudflare Web Analytics is also used; it sets no cookies. See the full Cookie Policy for the cookies used and how to change your choice or opt out.
