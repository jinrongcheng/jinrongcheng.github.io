COOKIE POLICY.

Last updated November 1, 2026.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored by your browser when you visit a website. They can remember preferences or help a site owner understand how the site is used. This policy explains the cookies used on jinrongcheng.ca.

Cookies this site uses

The site itself sets no cookies. The only cookies are from Google Analytics, and they are set only if you accept them in the cookie banner on your first visit:

  • _ga — up to 2 years, distinguishes visitors.
  • _gid — 24 hours, distinguishes visitors.
  • _ga_<container-id> — up to 2 years, keeps session state.
  • _gac_* — up to 90 days, relates to advertising campaigns (not used for advertising on this site).

These cookies collect anonymized usage information, such as pages visited and approximate location. They do not identify you by name, and they are not used to sell data or show ads.

Cloudflare Web Analytics

This site also uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies and does not collect personal data. It counts visits in aggregate only.

If you decline

If you decline in the banner, Google Analytics runs without storing analytics cookies, and you can still use the whole site. Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage (not a cookie) so the banner does not reappear on every visit.

Changing your choice

Clicking this clears your saved choice and reloads the page so the banner appears again. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics entirely with Google's opt-out browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.
